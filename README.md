SUJON ⚡ MASTER
Contractor Accounting App for Electric, Plumbing and Sanitary Work in Bangladesh
SUJON MASTER is an Android app for contractors who have many workers (mistri and helpers) and have to pay them weekly or monthly, and keep site payment accounts with PDF support.

Features
Daily Hisab — daily income and expense, with date picker
Team / Mistri-Helper Hisab — worker attendance (present on tap), days worked, monthly taken amount, hazira, OT (manual amount only), worker payment, monthly statement
Site Hisab — separate account for each site, amount received, site expense, received minus expense = net
Notes — notepad with photo draw and edit, text and straight lines on photos
Price List — electric, plumbing and sanitary material prices, with search and add new item
PDF Export — worker monthly statement PDF (1 page, English) and site received PDF (Bengali)
PIN Lock — 4-digit PIN lock in Settings
Offline Backup — JSON backup download and restore, Google Drive cloud backup with Gmail sign-in and auto-sync
Themes — Sunset, Ocean and Leaf themes, Bengali and English

Electric contractors
Plumbing contractors
Sanitary contractors
Any contractor in Bangladesh who manages mistri and helpers and site payments
